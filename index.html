About the scenery ↗

Photo-inspired pixel art of Chełm’s Basilica of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary. All ten levels are fictional routes. The underground is an imaginative chalk cave inspired by Chełm. View the reference photograph.

Level 3: Starostwo Powiatowe, plac Niepodległości 1. Building reference.

Level 4: Plac Łuczkowskiego at evening, inspired by a post-renovation photograph published again in October 2026. Square reference photograph.

Level 6: Chełmski Park Wodny, based on the tourism office exterior photograph published in February 2024; capture date unknown.

Level 7: Zorza Cinema, inspired by the current-façade photograph published in April 2026; capture date unspecified.

Level 8: Podziemia Kredowe entrance, inspired by the entrance-area photo published in August 2026; capture date unspecified. Courtyard composition is adapted for the game.

Level 9: Park Miejski im. Unii Europejskiej. Based on September 2026 event photos and the city’s wider photograph of the renovated park. Event installations and crowds omitted for the game.
