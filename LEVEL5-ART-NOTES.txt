TRAFFIC VEHICLE ART
Generated with the built-in image generation tool as an original sprite atlas.
Asset: assets/traffic-vehicles.png
Reference: blue/white/black Chelm NesoBus, photographed during Lublin testing.
https://www.dziennikwschodni.pl/artykul/372607,wodorowe-autobusy-trafily-na-lubelskie-ulice-to-testy-dla-chelma
https://static2.dziennikwschodni.pl/data/wysiwig/archive/media/news/2025/2025-03/bb45cc4f1bcdd6e41e911db069594227-v3.jpg
Photo used only as a visual reference; source photo is not included in the game.
Cars are unbranded designs inspired by Golf Mk5, BMW E46, Audi A4 B7 Avant,
and Mercedes W211 shapes from 2000-2010. No affiliation or endorsement implied.

Generation brief: production pixel-art vehicle sprite atlas on transparent
background for a 1990s-style Frogger game; five isolated right-facing vehicles,
long axes horizontal, elevated view with roofs and sides visible. NesoBus from
the supplied photo: blue lower panels and roof, white belt, black window band,
three doors, rooftop hydrogen housing and silver wheels. Four unbranded cars:
muted red compact hatchback, steel-blue sports sedan, silver-green estate,
champagne executive sedan. Period-appropriate headlights, trim, wheels, glass.
No logos, brand badges, text, license numbers, road, scenery or extra objects.
Game draws atlas regions at their native proportions and mirrors traffic for
the opposite direction. Road, tickets and street furniture are canvas artwork.
