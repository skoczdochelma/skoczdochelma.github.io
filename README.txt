CHEŁM — THE LIME TRAIL
16-bit edition · Ten connected chapters · Revision 94

PLAY OFFLINE
Extract the ZIP fully and open index.html in a modern browser. No installation or internet required. Alternatively open play-standalone.html, which embeds all game assets.

CONTROLS
Left/Right or A/D: move. Space: jump. Up/Down or W/S: climb ladders.
At a ladder top, Space or Up jumps; Down descends. P pauses; R retries.
On level 5, arrows/WASD move freely across the road. Watch for cars and cyclists.
Sound starts after Play; use the options menu to control music and effects.
The development links below the game start any level directly.

LEVELS
1. Old Town: red brick platforms, priests, 38 holy medallions.
2. Chalk Underground: chalk ledges, ghosts, 40 chalk pieces.
3. Starostwo: pavement platforms, office workers, 43 papers.
4. Plac Łuczkowskiego: evening scenery, timber platforms, tourists, 45 drinks.
5. Across Chełm: four traffic lanes, a safe median, outer cycle lanes, cyclists, scooters and 10 maps.
6. Chełmski Park Wodny: sunny aquapark scenery, turquoise tiled platforms, male lifeguards, 39 liferings, original lively summer soundtrack.

7. Zorza Cinema: glass-mosaic facade at dusk, metal lighting-truss platforms, movie directors, 42 film reels and an original cinematic score.

8. Podziemia Kredowe entrance: timber support beams, medieval mine workers and 41 tickets. Original gentle, mysterious soundtrack.

9. Park Miejski: final full-scale route, connected bench-plank platforms, tracksuit-clad male enemies, 37 leaves and calm nature-inspired music.

Collect all items and reach the gate (bus stop on level 5) to advance. Level 9 leads to the final Chalk Guardian encounter. The platform stages target roughly three minutes, depending on exploration and retries. They include dry pits, shaped platforms, horizontal/vertical movers and ladders. Flags save a midpoint respawn position. Items remain collected after losing a heart; retrying resets the stage. Each new stage restores three hearts. Progress lasts for the browser session.

The screen uses the established 3:2 presentation. Background ground strips scroll with the platforms. Moving-platform items keep stable animation phases. Music is synthesised and looped locally without downloads.

SCENERY
All routes are fictional. See LEVEL4-ART-NOTES.txt, LEVEL5-ART-NOTES.txt, LEVEL6-ART-NOTES.txt and TILESET-CREDITS.txt for references. LEVEL 10 — THE CHALK GUARDIAN
A dynamic, upbeat action score accompanies the fight.
The Park Miejski exit now leads into a compact final chamber.
Jump between eight stationary chalk platforms arranged at varied heights. No ladders.
Two chalk fragments appear on two randomly selected platforms for three seconds.
The next wave always uses two different platforms. Collect 10 fragments in total.
Eight small cave ghosts interrupt the bear. Collected chalk survives damage; retrying resets it.
The large guardian hovers at the right edge and falls into its matching-width pit when the tenth piece is collected.
Victory music loops through the fall and congratulations screen. START AGAIN? returns
to level 1. Victory music continues until restarting or closing the game. Audio toggles apply.
Use the temporary 10 · Chalk Guardian button to test the finale directly.

VISUAL POLISH
Startup overlays and moving-platform arrow labels removed. Level 1 has three pigeons;
level 2 has three flying and two hanging bats. Chalk shapes have rough undersides.
The level 5 finish pad pulses gently when ready and names the next destination.
Four white birds sit along Zorza’s roof; ginger and tuxedo cats sit over the tunnel
entrance; two squirrels move through the park background. These animals are decorative.

Finale soundtrack: Guardian’s March, a new 112 BPM lower-register boss theme.
The finale includes the cave’s three flying and two hanging bats, plus its complete
layered chalk ground texture. Platform and pit collision positions are unchanged.
