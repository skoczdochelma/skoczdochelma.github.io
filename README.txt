CHEŁM — THE LIME TRAIL
16-bit edition · Nine connected chapters · Revision 60

PLAY OFFLINE
Extract the ZIP fully and open index.html in a modern browser. No installation or internet required. Alternatively open play-standalone.html, which embeds all game assets.

CONTROLS
Left/Right or A/D: move. Space: jump. Up/Down or W/S: climb ladders.
At a ladder top, Space or Up jumps; Down descends. P pauses; R retries.
On level 5, arrows/WASD move freely across the road. Watch for cars and cyclists.
Sound starts after Play; use the options menu to control music and effects.
The development links below the game start any level directly.

LEVELS
1. Old Town: red brick platforms, priests, 39 holy medallions.
2. Chalk Underground: chalk ledges, ghosts, 39 chalk pieces.
3. Starostwo: pavement platforms, office workers, 39 papers.
4. Plac Łuczkowskiego: evening scenery, timber platforms, tourists, 39 drinks.
5. Ticket to Ride: four traffic lanes, a safe median, outer cycle lanes, six bus tickets.
6. Chełmski Park Wodny: sunny aquapark scenery, turquoise tiled platforms, male lifeguards, 39 liferings, original lively summer soundtrack.

7. Zorza Cinema: glass-mosaic facade at dusk, metal lighting-truss platforms, movie directors, 39 film reels and an original cinematic score.

8. Podziemia Kredowe entrance: timber support beams, medieval mine workers and 39 tickets using the level 5 artwork. Original gentle, mysterious soundtrack.

9. Park Miejski: final full-scale route, connected bench-plank platforms, tracksuit-clad male enemies, 39 leaves and calm nature-inspired music.

Collect all items and reach the gate (bus stop on level 5) to advance. Level 9 completes the adventure. The platform stages target roughly three minutes, depending on exploration and retries. They include dry pits, shaped platforms, horizontal/vertical movers and ladders. Flags save a midpoint respawn position. Items remain collected after losing a heart; retrying resets the stage. Each new stage restores three hearts. Progress lasts for the browser session.

The screen uses the established 3:2 presentation. Background ground strips scroll with the platforms. Moving-platform items keep stable animation phases. Music is synthesised and looped locally without downloads.
